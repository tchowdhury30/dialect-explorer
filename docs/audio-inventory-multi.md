# MULTI recording audit

The supplied MULTI ZIP contains 47 WAVs (181.7 minutes). The checked-in app already contained 464 processed phrase MP3s. The source map referenced six ZIP paths for 153 existing outputs, but a filename match alone does not establish that the source version is identical: ten mapped spans extend past the ends of two matching ZIP WAVs. Do not force-rebuild existing clips from this archive without reconciling the raw recording versions.

## Additions in this change

- Jordanian: Ghaina's “Do you have a map?” and Habib and Khalid's “Where is the police station?”
- Egyptian: Mario's “Where do you work?” and “Can you go back?” These recordings add words to the shared phrasing; the exact recorded variant is displayed next to Mario's button.
- Speaker introductions: Karim, Omar, Ali, Salim and Khalid. Their actual photos are used where available; cards without photos have no generated substitute.

The new phrase clips were selected by comparing timed Arabic ASR against the phrase text and comparing voice embeddings against existing speaker recordings, including held-out references. Each selected excerpt was independently transcribed with two speech-recognition models. Speaker and wording matches are evidence-based but are not a substitute for native-speaker listening. The app does not display unverified automatic bio transcripts as fact.

The source map records paths, offsets and SHA-256 checksums for each newly cut phrase clip and the Jordanian introductions. The audio builder checks a supplied checksum before rebuilding; it also accounts for Windows extraction removing trailing spaces from archive path components. The three Egyptian bio clips are generated by `scripts/build-speaker-bios.mjs`, which checks the source checksum. See `web-app/public/audio/bios/README.md` for offsets. Generated MP3s ship in the app and use versioned URLs.

## Remaining review

The archive contains repeated prompts, alternate takes, bilingual instruction, group discussion, and material that differs from the existing phrase wording. It has not been fully reviewed by a human or a native Arabic speaker. Other potential phrase takes were not added where speech, speaker attribution, wording, or clean boundaries could not be established with enough confidence. The raw WAVs stay outside Git. A further listening/diarization pass is needed before claiming every usable take has been added.
